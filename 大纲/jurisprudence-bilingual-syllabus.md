## 2. 课程说明（Course Description）

法理学是对法律的一般性哲学研究。它提出跨越一切具体法律体系的概念性与规范性追问：法律是什么？法律必须是强制性的吗？法律与道德之间是否存在必然联系？法律能否对其约束对象主张权威？法律与正义之间是何种关系？等等。一言以蔽之，法理学研究的是法律的第一性原理（复数）。

Jurisprudence is the philosophical study of law in general. It asks conceptual and normative questions that cut across all particular legal systems: What is law? Must law be coercive? Is there a necessary connection between law and morality? Can law claim authority over those it binds? What is the relationship between law and justice? In short, jurisprudence investigates the first principles of law.

本课程沿两条互补的线索展开。**概念线索**经由主要法学流派考察法律的本质、法律规范、法律推理与法律效力。**历史线索**将这些流派置于其思想脉络之中，追溯西方与中国法律理论自古及今的发展。

The course proceeds along two complementary tracks. The **conceptual track** examines the nature of law, legal norms, legal reasoning, and legal validity through the main schools of legal thought. The **historical track** situates those schools in their intellectual contexts, tracing the development of Western and Chinese legal theory from antiquity to the present.

法理学的最初学术传统来自于西方，但其覆盖范围早已超出了原产地。课程结束时，学生应能批判性地参与法理学的核心论争，将本国法律教义置于更广阔的理论框架中加以定位，并就法律的本质与目的构建并辩护有理据的论证。

The earliest scholarly tradition of jurisprudence is Western, but its reach has long extended beyond its place of origin. By the end of the course, students will be able to engage critically with the central debates of jurisprudence, to situate domestic legal doctrine within a broader theoretical framework, and to construct and defend reasoned arguments about the nature and purpose of law.

## 3. 学习成果（Learning Outcomes）

顺利完成本课程后，学生应能够：

On successful completion of the course, a student should be able to:

1. **识别并解释** 法理学的核心问题，以及主要法学流派给出的主要答案。

   **Identify and explain** the central questions of jurisprudence and the principal answers offered by the major schools of legal thought.
2. **区分并比较** 自然法理论、法律实证主义、法律现实主义、解释主义与批判法学进路，并援引其代表人物与文本。

   **Distinguish and compare** natural law theory, legal positivism, legal realism, interpretivism, and critical approaches to law, with reference to their leading proponents and texts.
3. **分析** 法律与道德、政治和社会之间的关系，并评价关于法律效力与法律权威的各种相互竞争的主张。

   **Analyse** the relationship between law, morality, politics, and society, and evaluate competing accounts of legal validity and legal authority.
4. **运用** 法理学理论处理具体的法律与社会问题，包括权利、正义、裁判与法治等问题。

   **Apply** jurisprudential theory to concrete legal and social problems, including questions of rights, justice, adjudication, and the rule of law.
5. **构建** 分析性法律哲学中清晰、论证充分且引注规范的论证，兼及口头与书面表达。

   **Construct** clear, well-reasoned, and properly referenced arguments in analytic legal philosophy, both orally and in writing.
6. **反思** 特定法律体系所生产的那些预设，并借助既有理论评价法律在社会中的意义。

   **Reflect** critically on the assumptions that particular legal systems produce, and appraise the significance of law in society by reference to established theory.

## 4. 教学与学习策略（Teaching and Learning Strategy）

本课程通过**课堂讲授**、**苏格拉底式研讨**、**指导性阅读**以及**案例与问题导向的讨论**相结合的方式实施。讲授引入理论框架与主要论点；研讨则要求学生围绕指定阅读材料中的既定问题展开，并在讨论中为自己的立场辩护。

The course is delivered through a combination of **lectures**, **Socratic seminars**, **guided reading**, and **case- and problem-based discussion**. Lectures introduce the theoretical framework and the primary arguments; seminars require students to work through set questions on the assigned readings and to defend positions in discussion.

学生应在每次课前完成指定阅读。每次研讨的参考准备时间约为三小时：一小时阅读原始文本，一小时阅读二手评注，一小时就讨论问题作书面准备。

Students are expected to complete the assigned reading before each session. Indicative preparation for each seminar is approximately three hours: one hour of primary-text reading, one hour of secondary commentary, and one hour of written preparation of the discussion questions.

**参与度考核**奖励有依据的贡献，而非单纯的出席。书面作业从论证、结构、材料运用与表达清晰度四方面评定。

**Assessment of participation** rewards informed contribution rather than mere attendance. Written work is assessed on argument, structure, use of sources, and clarity of expression.

All components must be attempted to pass the course. The mid-term essay develops the skills of close reading and argumentation; the final assessment tests cumulative understanding and independent critical judgement.

## 6. 课程大纲（Module Outline）

### 第一部分 · 基础（Part I — Foundations）

**1. 安提戈涅：法律的源初冲突** —— 索福克勒斯的《安提戈涅》作为本课程全部论争的起点：诸神的不成文法与君主敕令之间的碰撞，家庭与国家、良知与权威之间的碰撞。代表性解读：黑格尔论伦理冲突的悲剧与伦理实体（*Sittlichkeit*）；自然法与实证主义关于不成文法与主权命令的对立；努斯鲍姆论悲剧、审议与善的脆弱性；巴特勒论亲属关系、性别与国家；拉康论欲望与象征秩序；斯坦纳论该剧的接受史；批判性与女性主义解读（杜齐纳斯与沃林顿；卡瓦雷罗；霍尼格）；以及关于克瑞翁主权例外的政治神学解读。

**1. Antigone: The Founding Conflict of Law** — Sophocles' *Antigone* as the origin of the course's debates: the collision between the gods' unwritten law and the sovereign decree, between family and state, conscience and authority. Representative readings: Hegel on the tragedy of ethical collision and *Sittlichkeit*; the natural-law and positivist opposition of unwritten law to sovereign command; Nussbaum on tragedy, deliberation and the fragility of goodness; Butler on kinship, gender and the state; Lacan on desire and the symbolic order; Steiner on the play's reception; critical and feminist readings (Douzinas and Warrington; Cavarero; Honig); and political-theological readings of Creon's sovereign exception.

## 7. 课程政策（Policies）

- **学术诚信。** 所有提交的作业必须为学生本人独立完成（当然可以使用工具）。抄袭与未经许可的合作属严重学术违规，将依本校规定处理。

  **Academic integrity.** All submitted work must be the student's own (the use of tools is, of course, permitted). Plagiarism and unauthorised collaboration are serious academic offences and will be dealt with under the institution's regulations.
- **出勤。** 学生应按时出席讲授与研讨。研讨参与计入考核，缺席无法获得该项成绩。

  **Attendance.** Regular attendance at lectures and seminars is expected. Seminar participation is assessed and cannot be earned by absence.
- **迟交。** 除事先获批延期外，迟交作业依本校处罚规定处理。

  **Late submission.** Late work is subject to the institutional penalty scheme unless an extension has been granted in advance.
- **引注。** 书面作业须采用统一且获认可的法学引注格式（如 OSCOLA，或任课教师指定之格式）。

  **Referencing.** Written work must use a consistent, recognised legal citation style (e.g. OSCOLA or the style prescribed by the instructor).
